Genset ECU (25 kVA) — the whole circuit on one page

Every block with its real designators and values, joined by named nets. Kirloskar 3GK550ETA 4SR1 · Bosch MD1CS162 pinout · 4-layer board · drawn from docs/draw_system.py

1 Power input

Battery to the protected rail. Survives load dump (TVS 43 V), reverse battery (P-FET) and negative spikes (clamp). Pin 06 (EGR feed) is the same circuit as the injector feed: D108, Q104 SQM120P10, D109/D110, C109/C110, L104 10 µH, C115–C118 → VBAT_EGR.

Also on this sheet: C103 100 nF · C104 1 µF + R102 2.2 Ω damper · C105, C106 · EMI filter values from emi_filter.cir

3 MCU, supervisor and the kill path

If the MCU hangs, resets or the stop switch is pressed, GATE_KILL forces every injector, metering and EGR gate off in hardware.

Also on this sheet: C303–C312 100 nF across 3V3_MCU · SET0/SET1 R303/R304 0 Ω · J301 SWD

9 CAN

Two TCAN1042HGV transceivers, split termination 60 Ω + 60 Ω with 4.7 nF to ground.

Also on this sheet: C901, C902, C904, C905 100 nF

4 Injector stage

100 V boost (TPS40210) for the 18 A peak, battery for the 9–11 A hold. Drawn: bank A and cylinder 1. 110 V zener stops the boost in hardware; the MCU can stop it with BOOST_DIS.

5 Metering valve and EGR bridge

Metering: low-side PWM at 1 kHz with current sense (closed loop). EGR: four 100 V FETs, AUIRS2184 per leg; EN low = coast (safe).

Also on this sheet: EGR drivers U504/U505 AUIRS2184S (IN from EGR_IN1/IN2), gates R503–R506 470 Ω, bootstraps D502/D503 + C504/C506 1 µF · ~SD = EGR_SD: R508 1k from EGR_EN, R509 10k down, kill Q507 · U503 INA181A1 on R507 → ISNS_EGR (0.4 V/A)

2 Rails

5 V from the buck, 3.3 V from the LDO, three 5 V sensor supplies behind an over-voltage cut-off. SENSOR_5V_MON = 5V_MAIN through R207 10k / R208 15k.

Also on this sheet: U201: UVLO R201 75k / R202 24.9k, RON R203 31.6k, BST C203 2.2 nF, PGOOD R204 100k · U203: R209 100 Ω, R212 100k, R213 10 Ω, C208/C209 22 nF, C210 1 µF · C211 47 µF, C214/C217/C220 10 µF 50 V

7 Speed inputs

Crank VR → zero-cross comparator with hysteresis (edges from 0.21 V at cranking). Cam Hall open-collector → divider to 3.3 V.

10 Connector and how to read this page

Same name = same wire. A flag such as VBAT_PROT in one panel is connected to every other flag with that name. Values are the production netlist's (hw/, 23 Nov 2026). Prototype: the same circuits; MCU STM32G474, screw terminals instead of J1001.
